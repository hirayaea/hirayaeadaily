Weekly Topic Rotation

Monday
Cardiology Core
ECG, ACS, arrhythmias, heart failure. Aligns with what you see Monday–Tuesday on the unit.
Tuesday
Respiratory
Mechanical ventilation, lung function, A-a gradient, V/Q mismatch, ARDS.
Wednesday
Renal & Metabolic
AKI, electrolytes, acid-base, RRT. High MRCP weighting — don't skip.
Thursday
ITU Pharmacology
Vasopressors, inotropes, sedation, anticoagulation, antifibrinolytics. One drug class per session.
Friday
General Medical Drugs
Antihypertensives, diuretics, antiarrhythmics, antidiabetics, steroids, common prescribing pitfalls. Core MRCP pharmacology.
Saturday
MCQ Day
Passmedicine / OnExamination. Timed Qs only. Review wrong answers. No new reading.
Sunday A
Devices, Imaging & Transplant
ECMO, LVAD/RVAD, 2D echo, POCUS, immunosuppression, rejection, post-transplant infections.
Sunday B
Antibiotics
Mechanism, spectrum, resistance patterns, common ITU regimens (VAP, line sepsis, post-transplant prophylaxis). Rotate with Sunday A every other week.
Ground rules
  • 1 hour on a work day is enough — if done consistently, it beats occasional 3-hour binges.
  • Anki every single day. Even 10 minutes. Cards on the commute count.
  • Tai chi every morning — even just 10 min. The habit matters more than the duration.
  • Email once daily, fixed time. On long days and post-nights, skip it entirely.
  • Research belongs on off days and late-shift mornings — never squeezed into work-day study blocks.
  • Japanese and hobbies are not rewards you earn — they're maintenance. Schedule them non-negotiably.
  • Never study post-night. Sleep is consolidation. Skipping sleep to study is net negative.
  • Clinical exposure is revision. When you manage a vent patient, you're doing respiratory medicine.
  • Rotate the weekly topics loosely — don't reschedule the same topic twice because you missed it. Move on and come back.
